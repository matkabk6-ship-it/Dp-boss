# DP BOSS Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 4, 2026**

DP BOSS collects the account details you provide (such as an email address or phone number), profile information, content you create, subscription records, device and security events, and limited service analytics. We use this information to operate the social network, deliver messages and notifications, prevent abuse, process subscriptions, and meet legal obligations.

Posts, stories, profile fields, and activity are shown only to the audience selected by the member. Direct messages are available only to conversation participants and authorized systems that protect the service. Members can adjust visibility, notification, and discovery controls from Settings, block other members, report content, download applicable account data, or request deletion of their account.

We use service providers for hosting, payment processing, communications, and security. They may process data only to provide their services. We do not sell personal information. We disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect members, DP BOSS, or the public.

Media and account data are protected using access controls, encrypted transport, and monitoring. No system is completely secure; members should use a strong, unique password and enable available security protections. We retain data only as long as needed for the purposes above, except where law requires longer retention.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact **privacy@dpboss.app**. If you are under the minimum age required in your jurisdiction, do not use DP BOSS.
